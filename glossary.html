Words we use here.

A few terms come up a lot on this site. Here's what they actually mean, plainly.

Your structure

Node

Your community's home on the platform. Anchored to a Resident Association, CPF or CBO you already trust, not something built to replace it.

Cell

A smaller group of neighbours inside a Node, small enough that people actually know each other.

Cell Steward

A person your community trusts, who runs a Cell and acts on what the platform surfaces: who might need to meet whom.

Node Admin

The person from your own community structure who administers your Node and approves who's involved.

On the platform

Gifts Profile

What you fill in when you join: what you can offer, not just what you need.

Trade Exchange

Where offers and needs inside your Node meet.

Marketplace

Where a Node finds a verified Grounder for something it can't supply itself.

Outside help

Grounder

An outside organisation or practitioner, verified first, placed into a community on the community's terms, not theirs.

The six pillars

Water, Food, Health, Safety, Energy, Skills & Trade

The six things that fail first in a crisis, and the six categories every offer, need and listing on the platform falls under.
