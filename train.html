Train with us

Skills you keep. Work you choose.

Three training tracks for young people in our communities. Funded, so you don't pay. Licensed, so the skill is yours to use. Not a job promise, and we say that up front.

Tell us you're interestedHow it works

Three tracks.

Visual Storytelling

Learn graphic recording. Draw what a community says, live, in the room, so people can see their own story and their own strength. The practice comes from The San Scribe.

Community Network Weavers

Learn to connect people. Work out who needs to meet whom, and build those links on purpose. This is the track that helps outside organisations get trusted in a community, on the community's terms.

Innovation Workshops

Learn a method for solving problems together. Neuro Narrative Mapping takes a group from naming the problem to generating real options. We start with those two phases. The third is still being built.

The names of these tracks aren't final.

How it works. Straight.

Training is funded by grants, so learners don't pay. When you finish, ResilientSA licenses you to run the training and use the software. The licence is free.

You're not employed by ResilientSA, and there's no guaranteed income. You're a licensed practitioner. You choose which engagements to take, you're paid per engagement, and you're free to work for anyone else too.

Some of that work comes through ResilientSA: workshops in communities, and helping outside organisations land their programmes. Some you'll find yourself.

Where this stands.

There's no cohort yet, and the funding isn't confirmed. We're writing our safeguarding policy before we start, and learners under 18 will need a parent or guardian's consent. So there's no sign-up form yet. If you're interested, or you want to fund a cohort, tell us.
