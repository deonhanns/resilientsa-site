For communities

Your structure stays yours.

The Resident Association. The CPF. The CBO. You already hold your community's trust. ResilientSA plugs into that. It doesn't replace it.

Talk to usHow a Node begins

What you get.

A Node of your own.

Your community's home on the platform, anchored to your Resident Association or CPF.

Stewards you choose.

Cell Stewards are people your community trusts. We train them in connecting people. You approve them.

Programmes you choose.

Outside organisations can offer support, but only you decide who comes in.

What it costs.

Nothing. Communities pay nothing, ever. The training is funded. The organisations who want to work with you pay us, not you.

You stay in control.

Outside organisations are guests. They can't come in unless you accept them, and they reach your people only through the people you appoint.

Members don't need an ID number, an email address or bank details to join. Their information isn't sold, and it isn't used to sell to them.

How a Node begins.

1. A conversation.

We meet your committee. You decide whether this fits. No pressure, no forms.

2. You choose your people.

A Node Admin from your structure, and your first Cell Stewards.

3. We train and set up.

Your Stewards learn network weaving. We set up the Node and stay close while it starts.

We haven't done this yet.

No community is on the platform so far. Delft is where we hope to start. If you're first, you help shape it, and that's a real offer, not a line.

Tell us about your community.

Who you are, where you are, and what your committee is dealing with. We'll come and listen first.
